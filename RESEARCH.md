# Research notes for the owner

These notes use only the materials in `sources/`. They are not legal advice.

## 1. How does the directive define statutory interest for late payment?

It defines statutory interest as simple interest at a rate equal to the applicable reference rate plus at least eight percentage points. For a euro-area Member State, the reference rate is either the rate applied by the European Central Bank to its most recent main refinancing operations or the marginal rate resulting from its most recent variable-rate tender procedures.

**Source:** Source 1, Article 2(6)–(7)(a).

## 2. The owner's note says the EU maximum late fee is EUR 25. Is that right?

That maximum is not supported by the supplied directive extract. Where late-payment interest becomes payable, the directive says the creditor is entitled to a **minimum fixed sum of EUR 40** for recovery costs. It also describes possible reasonable recovery-cost compensation above that fixed sum. The extract does not state a EUR 25 maximum. This is a recovery-cost compensation rule, not a general permission to add an arbitrary fee to a reminder.

**Source:** Source 1, Article 6(1)–(3).

## 3. Does the business have to send a reminder before it is entitled to interest and the fixed compensation sum?

No reminder is required under the supplied text: for qualifying business-to-business transactions, the creditor is entitled to late-payment interest without a reminder, provided the creditor fulfilled its obligations and did not receive payment on time (unless the debtor is not responsible for the delay). The fixed recovery-cost sum is also payable without the necessity of a reminder, where the directive's conditions for late-payment interest are met.

**Source:** Source 1, Article 3(1)(a)–(b) and Article 6(1)–(2).

## 4. What is the current ECB reference rate?

**Not in the sources.** The provided extract identifies the kind of ECB rate used and says the rate in force on 1 July applies to the second half of the year, but it does not give the current numeric rate. To answer with a value, the owner would need the relevant official ECB rate publication for the applicable period.

**Source:** Source 1, Article 2(7)(a) and Article 3(2)(b); no numeric rate is provided in the source files.
